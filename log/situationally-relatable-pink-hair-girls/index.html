situationally relatable pink hair girls

2026-06-11
i don't usually relate to female characters but when i do they're pink.

uzuki yashiro (the world ends with you)

it feels like being pissed off used to be my main emotion but i can't remember the last time i felt that way.

uzuki was relatable for how high strung and perfectionist and irritated she constantly was. and since it's TWEWY everyone's so stylish!

amu hinamori (shugo chara!)

she's the school's cool girl who's actually very shy and has trouble showing her true personality. the first episode felt so relatable it was kinda embarrassing since she's like eight years old. i literally had the social issues of a grade schooler as a 22 year old man. i think that was largely a result of being on social media where posting anything publicly felt like hell.

miwako sakurada (paradise kiss)

her mental illness and how she copes with it were very relatable. i felt like i had to steel myself through my thoughts by believing i lived in a lovely cutey sweetie bear wonderland just like how she surrounds herself with cute things and carries candy 'medicine' that makes her feel better. i still feel like this to some extent. i call my antidepressants my happy bear pills and my ADHD meds my focused bear pills. i love how much hiroyuki cares for her. i truly despise arashi but at the same time i understand her response to him.

when i think about it miwako seems like she would be popular with menhera girls but she's so earnest and sweet that people don't treat her that way.

aster (NU:carnival)

he's not a situationally relatable pink hair girl but he is a situationally relatable pink hair guy. don't be fooled by his cute appearance!
