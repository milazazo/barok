monthly recap (november 2024)

2024-11-30

i was in hospital and recovering from home so the month flew by. i feel like i spent most of the time just chatting to people on dating apps. i've also been extremely fixated on local pro wrestling to the point where i feel like i'm going crazy.

bear cookies

i baked some bear-shaped cookies. when my baby niece saw a picture, she got so obsessed with them that she lost appetite to eat until i gave one to her after lunch.

art log

drew genbu and eishin for the sideM halloween collab, plus a yamu for the idolmaster fanbook.

media log

  • impact pro wrestling - grave consequences: TBH i think pro wrestling is lowkey rotting my brain.
  • what happens next: i came across it in some kinda toxic internet zine on itch.io and read it all in one night. it's suchhh an interesting webcomic but the subject matter is so triggering that i can't recommend it to anyone who might actually be interested in the topic.
  • mouthwashing: the art and storytelling is really good but i can't fathom how something so short and about such a serious subject matter managed to get such a huge fandom. i'm a fan of jun oda from yakuza so i guess it's not even a case of the subject matter but more the length. the game is two hours long! it's like making AUs and acrylic charms and silly fanart of black swan or helter skelter!
  • owl criticism - euthanasia fan fiction: i've heard criticism of yanagihara's works before but this one is interesting in that it defines what trauma porn is and how a little life isn't that due to the author's own old-school beliefs about therapy.
  • persona 2 eternal punishment - sewers: i guess i'm making the game a lot more tedious than it needs to be. didn't particularly care to get all the new dialogue from the innocent sin kids but it's also such a pain to travel around every store in the city to get the most of the eternal punishment adults.
  • idolmaster xenoglossia E1-E2: it's interesting enough. none of the characters are too different aside from yayoi who has fuck all to do with her actual self.
