monthly recap (february 2025)

2025-03-13

it's like i was unreal for 21 years, started existing at 22, and started living at 23. i'm not used to being alive so it's scary.

most of my friends have received their cards now, so here are all of the characters i drew! i was procrastinating editing this for so long that i only got around to posting the recap now.

MBTI bullshit

got stupidly invested in MBTI for a week. usually i just get INFP so i was surprised that i got ENTP in sakinorva's test. i thought there might be an extroversion bias so i DMed a bunch of my introverted friends to do the test (this may actually be the most ENTP behavior).

sakinorva test seems to be split into grant function and myers function which are two diff calculations based on different peoples' personality theories (the myers function is essentially MBTI but they can't call it that for copyright reasons). i ordered it like so: 16personalities - sakinorva grant function / sakinorva myers function

  • INFP (me) - ENTP / ENTP
  • INTP 1 - ENTP / INFP
  • INTP 2 - INTP / INTP
  • INTP 3 - ENTP / INTP
  • INFJ - ISTJ / INFJ
  • INFP - ISFJ / INFJ
  • INFP 2 - ENTP / INFP
  • INFP 3 - INFP / INFP
  • ISFJ - ISTJ / ISFJ

basically i think there's something wrong with me.

i also thought that 16personalities has a very strong 'feeling' bias, but people who got feeling in that test tended to get feeling here (or the same overall MBTI) in general so i am not sure what i'm doing to get the complete opposite of that test lmao. i made my parents do 16personalities as well and they got INTJ (dad) and ISTJ (mom). what growing up in communist china does to a mf...

people who are deeper into MBTI make distinction between being Extroverted in itself (which i am not really) and having Extroverted ways of thinking (which i guess i do?) but in that case the term is just misleading. i think i'm just someone who despises misinformation and finds other people so incredibly interesting even though my memory is too bad to remember anything about them lol.

laneway 2025

charli xcx came to NZ for the laneway festival! the tickets were $230 NZD and her set was 1 hour long so i decided to hang out at a nearby spot with good visibility. that spot was blocked off by security so i went to the nearby park instead. the sound across the lake was really clear.

there were surprisingly few people loitering around aside from me and one other guy sitting at the same picnic bench. the guy was a 40+ y/o househusband who didn't know of charli but stopped to listen to the festival while walking his dog. i spent most of the time drawing, but we struck up a bit of a convo. it was fun to interact with someone else during a concert for once.

media log

  • the weeknd - trilogy: dawn fm is one of my favorite male albums so i wanted to listen to all of the weeknd's previous songs before the new release of hurry up tomorrow. i was surprised that the trilogy's tracks were also seamless, but didn't care for the music itself. my favorite song was D.D and that's a cover.
  • sabrina carpenter - short n sweet deluxe: someone described 15 minutes as a mix of fast times, feather, and juno— and as someone who has those songs as my three faves of hers i was instantly hooked!!! busy woman has grown on me a lot. i really like please please please with dolly parton as well. and fuck barry keoghan!
  • antoine de saint-exupéry - the little prince: i can't believe it was a children's book. it's so sad...
  • central cee - wild west, 23, can't rush greatness: CRG was fine but i'm not going back to it anytime soon. i'm sick of lines that are like "if you don't want to be with me you're just gay". it's fine when sabrina does it though because i genuinely believe any man who doesn't want to be with her is homosexual.
  • the weeknd - kiss land, beauty behind the madness, starboy, my dear melancholy, after hours, dawn fm, hurry up tomorrow: admittedly i never understood what people meant by the weeknd being a main pop boy before but the transition between kiss land and starboy is crazy. rockin' is so fucking good. i actually don't care for after hours that much outside of blinding lights but dawn fm, again, is one of my top male vocalist albums. it's a shame it never got the commercial longevity it deserved. i certainly listened to hurry up tomorrow but i don't know if i caught anything from it so i'll give it another go later.
